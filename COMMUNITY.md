# Community Updates

We share roadmap progress and gather feedback from library consumers on a lightweight, regular cadence.

## Monthly update thread

- On the **first week of each month**, a maintainer opens a post in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/dark-princezz/stellar-hooks/discussions) titled
  `Community Update — <Month YYYY>`.
- Each update covers:
  - ✅ What shipped last month (releases, merged hooks)
  - 🚧 What is in progress (current milestone from [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md))
  - 🙋 Where help is wanted (`good first issue`, `help wanted`, [bounties](BOUNTIES.md))
  - 💬 Open questions for consumers
- Feedback is collected as replies to the thread. An optional community call may be scheduled
  when there are larger topics to discuss; the link is posted in the thread.

## Cross-posting to the Stellar community

Major releases and roadmap updates are cross-posted to the
[Stellar Developer Discord](https://discord.gg/stellardev) (e.g. `#dev-help` / showcase channels)
to attract testers and contributors.

When cross-posting:

1. Link to the release notes / [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and the monthly update thread.
2. Link to the [public roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and open `good first issue` items.
3. Keep it short — a 2–3 line summary plus links.

### Template

```
📦 stellar-hooks <version> is out!
<one-line highlight>
Release notes: <link> · Roadmap: https://github.com/dark-princezz/stellar-hooks/blob/main/ROADMAP.md
Looking for testers & contributors: https://github.com/dark-princezz/stellar-hooks/labels/good%20first%20issue
```
